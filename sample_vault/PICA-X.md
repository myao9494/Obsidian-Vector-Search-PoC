# PICA-X (Phenolic-Impregnated Carbon Ablator)

## 1. Overview
PICA-X is a lightweight carbon-phenolic composite ablative material developed for atmospheric entry spacecraft.
It is manufactured by infiltrating a rigid porous carbon fiber preform with a phenolic resin system.

## 2. Thermal Response and Pyrolysis Model
The thermal response of PICA-X is strongly affected by resin decomposition at elevated temperatures.
The pyrolysis model describes the thermal decomposition of the phenolic resin matrix into low molecular weight gases and residual carbonaceous char.
The resulting gases contribute significantly to the internal pressure buildup and flow through the porous matrix.

## 3. Surface Recession and Oxidation
During peak hypersonic heating, surface oxidation and sublimation occur.
The recession rate depends on the local heat flux, stagnation pressure, and chemical boundary layer conditions.
